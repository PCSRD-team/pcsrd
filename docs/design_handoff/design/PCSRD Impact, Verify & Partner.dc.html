08 — OFFICIAL CHANNELS · /verify PRIORITY 2 OF THE PRODUCT

This page carries a real safety function, so it is designed against how it is actually used: someone receives a suspicious message on WhatsApp and wants to check it in seconds, on a phone, possibly on a poor connection. Three decisions follow. The channel registry is a table of values, not prose. There is a share card — a bordered plate sized to be screenshotted and forwarded, because forwarding a screenshot is how this information travels here. And the fraud report form sits on the same page, so the person who has just discovered an impersonator does not have to navigate anywhere to report it.
