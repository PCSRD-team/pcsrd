00 — DIRECTION PCSRD · INSTITUTIONAL WEBSITE · AR/EN
SELECTED DIRECTION

The Record.

السِّجِل

The site is designed as an institutional record rather than a campaign. A due-diligence officer reads it to check whether claims hold up; a family in Gaza reads it to find out what they qualify for and which channel is genuinely ours. Both are served by the same quality: everything stated is stated with its source visible.

So the interface is built from ruled paper, ink and one marking colour — not from cards, gradients and shadows. Structure is drawn with lines that carry meaning. Photography appears as documented evidence, captioned and credited, never as decorative wallpaper behind a headline.

P1
Evidence over emphasis

Every published number renders locked to its period and status. Provenance is a visible design element, not a footnote.

P2
Ruled, not floated

Hierarchy comes from rules and column structure. Three line weights carry fixed meaning. Cards exist only where an image is intrinsic to the content.

P3
Arabic sets the measure

Line height, type size and rhythm are tuned to Arabic first; the Latin cut adapts to it. RTL is the default drawing, LTR the mirror — not the other way round.

P4
Two doors, always open

Partnership and channel verification are the two jobs the site exists for, so both live in persistent chrome on every route — not only on their own pages.

P5
Paper, ink, one mark

Warm paper, navy ink. Gold is a marking colour only — the rule under a heading, the attested stamp, the focus ring. It is never a fill, never a gradient.

CONSIDERED AND REJECTED
Humanitarian photojournalism

Full-bleed portrait heroes with an overlaid plea. Reads as fundraising, competes with the due-diligence job, and risks dignity harm under the do-no-harm rules.

CONSIDERED AND REJECTED
Data-dashboard NGO

Counters, donut charts, animated totals. The organisation holds few verified figures; a dashboard shell implies a measurement apparatus that does not exist yet.

CONSIDERED AND REJECTED
Institutional-blue template

Blue hero, three feature cards, logo carousel. Indistinguishable from every other NGO site — and indistinguishable is the exact failure mode for an organisation being impersonated.

CONTENT NOTE

Per §9 of the build brief, no facts about the organisation are invented here. UI chrome, labels and Arabic microcopy are real and ready to lift into the dictionaries. Organisational facts — legal name, licence number, figures, donor names, dates — appear as bracketed placeholders or are tagged SAMPLE, at realistic lengths so the layout is tested honestly. Photography is a droppable slot until real field images with consent references arrive.

The specced navy and gold ramps are kept verbatim. Three tokens are added: a warm paper ground in place of pure white, a warm hairline, and a warm alt surface. 1.3 · RULE SEMANTICS

Three weights, fixed meanings. A line in this system always says something.

2px ink — section opens here
Top of a major block. Never decorative.
2px gold, 88px — attested
Marks a verified figure, an official channel, a signed statement.
1px warm hairline — separation
Between rows and fields. Carries no emphasis.
3px programme accent, 56px
The only place a programme colour appears at size.
RADIUS & ELEVATION

Radius 0 on structural surfaces. 6px on controls and inputs only. No shadow anywhere on the public site — depth is carried by the 1px rule and the paper/alt contrast. Shadow returns only for genuinely floating admin layers: dialog, sheet, dropdown.
